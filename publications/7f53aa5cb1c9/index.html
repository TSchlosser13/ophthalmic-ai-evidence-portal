Deep learning architectures analysis for age-related macular degeneration segmentation on optical coherence tomography scans

K Alsaih, M Z Yusoff, T B Tang, I Faye, F M\'eriaudeau

Computer methods and programs in biomedicine

2020Journal articleAge-related macular degenerationRetinal fluid / oedema
Year2020
Structured terms13

Interpretive overview

Contribution summary

01

The authors compare four widely used segmentation architectures and show that patching plus fine-tuning improves dense fluid delineation, yielding high mean Dice scores (up to 0.85) that support more accurate OCT-based AMD fluid segmentation.

Overall reasoning

Using deep learning CNN-based semantic segmentation on OCT scans, the authors delineate three AMD-associated retinal fluid types, applying patch-based training on the RETOUCH challenge data and testing generalisation via OPTIMA, with evidence reported using Dice similarity metrics versus human performance.
