Rights and responsible reuse

Licence and content note

The portal brings together public metadata, derived classifications, authored figures, and external links whose reuse conditions may differ.

Read each layer on its own terms

Availability through this portal does not, by itself, create a universal reuse licence. Check the relevant rights statement before redistributing code, data products, figures, or commentary.

01

Website code

A project owner may apply an open-source licence to the website code. Until such a licence is explicitly added by the repository owner, no additional code-reuse licence is implied.

02

Public metadata and classifications

Bibliographic metadata, dataset records, and evidence assignments remain subject to the licence selected by the review authors and any applicable database rights.

03

Authored figures and commentary

Authored figures and interpretive commentary remain the work of the listed authors and may also be subject to journal submission or publication terms.

04

External publications

External publication links and bibliographic metadata remain subject to their respective terms. No third-party publication content is redistributed by the portal.

This page is a content-boundary summary rather than legal advice. Where specific licence text is supplied with an individual artefact, that text governs the artefact.
